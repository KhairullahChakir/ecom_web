About ShopDemo

Understanding our AI-powered analytics platform

Our Mission

ShopDemo is a demonstration e-commerce website built to showcase the power of AI-driven analytics in online retail. We use real-time machine learning models to predict customer behavior and reduce cart abandonment.

How Tracking Works

Every page you visit is classified by type (Product, Administrative, Informational) and tracked in real-time. Our AI monitors your browsing patterns using a Temporal Convolutional Network (TCN) to predict exit intent. If the AI detects you might leave without purchasing, it triggers a personalized intervention with a smart discount.

Technology Stack

Our system runs on FastAPI backends serving ONNX-optimized models. The TabM model predicts purchase intent from session features, while the TCN model analyzes page sequences for exit prediction. All data is stored in MariaDB with real-time analytics served via REST APIs.

Privacy & Transparency

This is a research demonstration. All tracking is visible to you through the tracker badge at the bottom of each page. No personal data leaves this demo environment. The system is designed to show how AI can improve e-commerce experiences while maintaining transparency.
